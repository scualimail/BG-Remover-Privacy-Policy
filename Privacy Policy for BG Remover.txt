**Privacy Policy for BG Remover**
*   **No Data Collection**: This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.
*   **On-Device Processing**: All image processing (background removal) happens entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to a server or seen by us.
*   **Permissions**: The camera permission is only used to take photos within the app. The storage permission is only used to save your edited images to your gallery.
*   **No Third Parties**: We do not share any information with third parties.
